## Modern travel performance, retrofit dimensions

European-design end carriages reduce wheel load and noise while improving positioning accuracy. This unit is built for crane bridges that need modern travel performance without changing the runway.

## Made to match

Each carriage is machined after fabrication so wheel centre distance, rail gauge and mounting hole pattern match the existing bridge. For new builds we supply the complete bridge and carriage package.

## Options

- VFD or contactor control
- IP55 or IP66 motors
- Polyurethane or forged steel wheels
- Integrated laser anti-collision sensor
- Custom paint and tagging

## Scope of supply

- End carriage weldment
- Wheel blocks, bearings and axles
- Travel drive and brake
- Buffers and rail sweeps
- Dimensional inspection report
