## Localised lifting without floor space

Wall-mounted jib cranes bring lifting power to a single workstation without occupying floor area or requiring a dedicated runway. The 5 Ton unit suits machine shops, welding cells and maintenance bays where a full EOT crane is not justified.

## Installation considerations

The wall bracket and tie-back transfer load into the building column. We review the column capacity and anchor details before supply, and can provide a freestanding pillar or one-side-leg design where the structure cannot take the load.

## Configurations

- Wall-mounted with 270° slewing
- Pillar-mounted with 360° slewing
- One-side-leg semi-gantry for longer travel paths
- Manual or motorised slewing
- Chain hoist or wire rope hoist

## Scope of supply

- Jib arm, slewing bearing and wall bracket
- Tie-back assembly and anchor bolts
- Hoist and trolley
- Pendant control and cabling
- Installation and load test
