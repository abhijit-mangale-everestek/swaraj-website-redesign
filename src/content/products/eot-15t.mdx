## Engineered for daily production duty

The 15 Ton EOT crane is our most requested overhead travelling crane. It is designed around a double girder bridge that carries a compact hoist trolley between the girders, giving a high hook approach and a short headroom envelope.

Every crane is configured to the building: span, lift height, runway rail size, power supply and control method. Structural members are fabricated in-house, blasted and primed before assembly, then load-tested with calibrated weights.

## Built around your bay

The standard 22.5 m span suits typical industrial sheds. For narrower bays we supply single girder variants from 1T to 10T, and for longer spans the bridge is re-engineered with additional stiffening and a matched end carriage.

## Scope of supply

- Crane bridge, end carriages and hoist trolley
- Runway conductor system, festoon and cable trolleys
- Pendant station with emergency stop, plus optional radio remote
- Commissioning, operator briefing and documentation
- Optional annual maintenance contract and spares programme
