## A hoist package that drops into your system

This 10 Ton wire rope hoist is designed as a drop-in lifting unit for monorail beams, single girder cranes and custom structures. The low headroom housing keeps the hook close to the beam, maximising usable lift height.

## Duty-matched components

The drum, rope, gearbox and brake are selected for the actual duty cycle rather than nominal capacity alone. For high-start-count applications we specify a larger frame size and VFD control to limit thermal load.

## Options

- IP66 protection and stainless hardware
- Flameproof motor and brake for hazardous areas
- Radio remote with dual-speed control
- Load display and data logging
- Anti-sway control for precision placement

- Hoist with grooved drum and wire rope
- Trolley and mounting bracket
