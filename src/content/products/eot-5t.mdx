## Right-sized overhead lifting

The 5 Ton single girder EOT crane is the entry point to overhead travelling cranes. It suits workshops where a double girder crane would be over-specified, and where headroom is limited.

## Configured to the building

Span, lift height and runway rail are confirmed from a site survey. The crane can be supplied with a complete runway structure or matched to an existing runway.

## Scope of supply

- Single girder bridge and end carriages
- Wire rope hoist and trolley
- Pendant station and cabling
- Runway conductor system
- Load test certificate and O&M manual
