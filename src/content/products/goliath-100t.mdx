## High-capacity gantry lifting

The 100 Ton Goliath crane is engineered for yards where loads, spans and duty cycles exceed the practical range of an overhead crane. The structure is sized from the actual load cases rather than a nominal capacity table.

## Site-led engineering

We review rail foundations, wheel loads, wind exposure and transport access before releasing drawings. Bridge sections are bolted for road transport and assembled on site by our erection team.

## Scope of supply

- Goliath bridge, legs and bogies
- Ground rail system and fasteners
- Hoist trolley and duty-matched rope
- Cabin and radio control system
- Erection, load testing and operator training
