## Lifting where a full gantry will not fit

One-side-leg cranes solve a common site problem: an overhead runway exists on one side of the bay, but the other side has no structure to support a bridge. The crane uses the existing runway on one end and a ground rail on the other.

## Survey-led design

We measure the existing runway, rail level and column positions, then design the leg and ground rail to suit. Civil foundation loads are issued to your contractor before fabrication.

## Scope of supply

- Bridge and end carriages
- Fabricated leg and bogie
- Ground rail and fasteners
- Hoist and trolley
- Control system, commissioning and load test
