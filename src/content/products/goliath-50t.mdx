## Gantry lifting for open yards

The 50 Ton Goliath crane is a rail-mounted gantry system built for yards where an overhead runway is not practical. Two fabricated legs carry the bridge across the working span, with motorised travel along ground rails.

## Configured to the site

Span, lift height, rail gauge and cantilever overhang are set from the site survey. For outdoor operation we specify wind bracing, rail clamps and storm anchors, and calculate the structure for local wind loads.

## Transport and erection

Bridge sections are bolted for road transport and assembled on site. Our erection team handles rail alignment, structural torque checks, electrical termination and load testing before handover.

## Scope of supply

- Goliath bridge, legs and bogies
- Ground rail system and rail clips
- Hoist trolley with duty-matched wire rope
- Cabin, pendant or radio control station
- Erection, load testing and operator training
