## The constraint

Precast units up to 85 tonnes had to move from casting beds to trailers on a site with soft ground and coastal wind exposure.

## What we supplied

A 100 tonne rail-mounted goliath crane with a 40 m span, engineered wheel loads, storm anchors, wind bracing and a cabin control station. Foundation and rail load drawings were issued to the civil contractor.

## Result

Finished precast units are now loaded directly onto transport, reducing dependence on hired heavy lift equipment.
