## The constraint

The press shop needed a reliable crane but could not absorb a long installation shutdown. The existing runway and headroom were fixed constraints.

## What we supplied

A 15 tonne double girder EOT crane configured to the existing runway, with VFD long travel, dual hoist brakes, overload limiting and a pendant station. The crane was pre-assembled and load-tested before dispatch.

## Result

Installation and commissioning were completed in a two-day window, with full capacity restored and modern safety systems in place.
