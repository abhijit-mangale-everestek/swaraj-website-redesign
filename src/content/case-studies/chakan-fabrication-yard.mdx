## The constraint

Anand Steel Structures needed to move heavy fabricated girders around a constrained outdoor yard. Mobile crane hire was expensive and scheduling depended on availability.

## What we supplied

A 50 tonne rail-mounted goliath crane with a 30 m span, storm anchors, walkways and a cabin plus radio control system. The ground rail and foundation loads were designed alongside the crane.

## Result

Routine yard handling is now in-house, with measurable savings in handling time and hire costs.
