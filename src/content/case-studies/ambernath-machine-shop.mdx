## The constraint

Machined components were being moved manually between CNC machines and inspection, with no practical location for a full overhead crane.

## What we supplied

Three 5 tonne wall-mounted jib cranes with 270° slewing, wall brackets and tie-backs, installed after verifying column capacity.

## Result

Lifting now happens at the machine, removing manual handling from the cell and improving changeover flow.
